Using Snufflewind

How exactly does it keep my dog busy for three hours?

The carousel holds 12 pockets. Winding the crown loads the spring; as it unwinds, one pocket rotates over the spout roughly every 12 minutes. The food drops onto the silicone tray below, where your dog has to nose and lick it out of the mat.

The last pocket opens at around 2 hours 24. Checking the tray, re-checking it, and settling down next to it usually fills the third hour. Every dog is different, but that is the rhythm we designed for.

Will my dog get bored of it?

Because the food arrives slowly, there is no single "solved" moment. Most dogs check back repeatedly between pockets. If yours learns the pattern, swap the difficulty ring — the tray arrangement changes, so the puzzle feels different without buying anything new.

Rotating the treats helps too: a few pieces of carrot, some kibble, a broken biscuit.

Is it safe to leave my dog alone with it?

Snufflewind is made for solo time, and there are no batteries, cables or small loose parts. That said, no toy is indestructible. Start with short sessions while you are in the next room, use the Easy ring, and check the tower is still upright when you get back.

If your dog is a serious chewer, use it during supervised sessions and take the tray away when it is empty.

What treats work best?

Dry kibble, small training treats, or broken-up biscuits — anything that fits through the spout without crumbling. Keep pieces around the size of a pea to a small bean. Avoid sticky or greasy food, which can gum up the carousel, and skip anything your dog should not eat.

My dog eats too fast. Will this help?

That is exactly what the tray is for. Food lands in the sniff mat rather than a bowl, so it takes nosing and licking to get it out. Use the Hard ring and slightly larger pieces to slow things down further. Remember to subtract the treats from your dog's daily food so they are not overfed.

Can two dogs use it together?

One station per dog works best. Sharing can cause guarding, especially if one dog is faster. If you have two dogs, set up two stations in different rooms, or use Snufflewind as a one-dog-at-a-time activity.

Is it suitable for puppies and senior dogs?

Yes, with the Easy ring and a bit of supervision. Puppies should always be supervised while they learn how it works, and senior dogs may prefer the shallower Easy ring if they have trouble reaching into the mat. If your dog has dental or mobility issues, check with your vet first.

Care & cleaning

How do I clean the tray?

Lift the tray out, tip any crumbs into the bin, and wash it on the top rack of the dishwasher or by hand in warm soapy water. Rinse and let it air-dry before the next use. The silicone is food-grade and does not hold onto smells the way plastic does.

Can the whole thing go in the dishwasher?

No — only the silicone tray and the three difficulty rings. The tower contains the stainless clockwork and should never be submerged. Wipe the tower and spout with a damp cloth and mild soap, then dry it straight away.

A treat is stuck. What should I do?

Let the mechanism run down, lift off the tray, and use a wooden chopstick or a soft brush to ease the piece out of the spout. Do not use metal tools or force the carousel — the movement is sturdy but not invincible. A quick wipe afterwards keeps everything moving freely.

How should I store it?

Let it run down, clean and dry it, and keep it somewhere dry and out of direct sun. Do not store it fully wound for months at a time; the spring prefers to rest. The tray can stay fitted, or be stored flat in the box.

Can I use it outdoors?

Yes, on a flat, dry surface such as a patio or a picnic blanket. It is not weatherproof, so bring it in if it rains. On grass, check the tray is level so treats land where your dog can reach them.

What if my dog chews the tower?

Snufflewind is a foraging toy, not a chew toy. The tower is BPA-free and tough, but teeth can mark it. Use it during supervised sessions, and if your dog likes to gnaw, offer a proper chew toy alongside it. Chewing damage is not covered by the warranty, but replacement parts are available.

Sizes & setup

Which size should I choose?

Go by weight: Small for dogs under 10 kg, Medium for 10–25 kg, Large for over 25 kg. If your dog is right on the boundary, choose the larger size — a bigger tray is easier to forage from than a cramped one. The tower height and treat capacity scale with the tray.

How do I swap the difficulty rings?

Lift out the tray, press the ring gently at one edge until it pops free, then press the new ring into the groove until it sits flush. No tools, no small screws, nothing to lose. Each station comes with Easy, Medium and Hard; the refill set adds a second of each.

How loud is the clockwork?

Quieter than a kitchen timer and much quieter than a squeaky toy. You will hear a soft tick and the little tumble of treats landing. It should not disturb a household, a phone call or a sleeping cat.

Does it need batteries, wifi or an app?

None of the above. The movement is a stainless steel clockwork spring, wound by hand. It works in a power cut, in a caravan, and in a garden with no signal. There is no firmware to update and no account to create.

How much food does it hold?

About 90 g in the Small, 140 g in the Medium and 200 g in the Large. That is a full meal for many dogs, so adjust their other meals on the days you use it. The pockets are measured for dry food, not wet.

What is the refill ring set for?

Every station already includes one Easy, one Medium and one Hard ring. The $18 refill set adds a second set of three, which is handy if you have two dogs of different abilities, want to keep one ring clean while another is in the dishwasher, or simply want a spare.
