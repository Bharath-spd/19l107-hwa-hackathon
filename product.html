Care & cleaning

  • Lift out the tray and wash it on the top rack of the dishwasher, or by hand in warm soapy water.
  • Wipe the tower and spout with a damp cloth. Never submerge the tower or put the clockwork in water.
  • Wind until you feel the stop, then stop. Forcing the crown past the stop is the only way to hurt it.
  • Let the mechanism run down before storing it for long periods. Keep it dry and out of direct sun.
